      How we handle your figures

      Worked out on your device

      Everything you type, and any bank statements you upload, is processed in your own browser. Statements are never uploaded. Only the monthly totals are used.

      Nothing is shared unless you choose to

      Your details only go to a broker when you press send, and only to the broker you pick. If your broker saves your figures, they're kept in that broker's private client list.

      Independent

      Suburb Fit isn't a bank or lender, and isn't owned by a broker group, real estate agency or property portal. We don't sell loans.

      Free for buyers, and honest about how we're paid

      Buyers never pay. Brokers may pay to be listed or to receive enquiries. That never changes your numbers, which use the same public data for everyone.

      Where the numbers come from

      Public 2026-27 sources: SA Valuer-General sales, council rates, SA Water, RevenueSA, the AER energy price, the ATO and federal home buyer schemes. Results are estimates and general information, not credit advice.
