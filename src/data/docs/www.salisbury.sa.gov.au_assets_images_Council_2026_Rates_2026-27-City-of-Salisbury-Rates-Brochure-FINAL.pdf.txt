Our priorities for 2026/27
Council’s ongoing focus on core business such
as road resealing, parks maintenance, and flood
management sit side-by-side with a progressive
agenda to deliver new assets for the community.

Our 2026/27 Annual Business Plan outlines priorities
for the year ahead, including greater investment in
sport and recreation and the exciting City Centre
redevelopment to deliver affordable housing, access
to community services, retirement living, new retail
and office spaces, and a multi-deck car park.
